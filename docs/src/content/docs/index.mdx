## Prompts are the source code

Other distros hand you their config. Stack OS hands you the *why*. Every change is recorded as the prompt that asked for it, the reason behind it and the exact diff it made. Paste the prompts into your own AI and you get the same OS, or your own twist on it.

<CardGrid stagger>
  <Card title="Make it yours in minutes" icon="pencil">
    Fork Stack OS, keep the prompts you like, write the ones you need. Your AI edits the blueprint, not your running machine, so every change survives reinstalls and reaches every computer you own.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Drop any opinion" icon="random">
    Don't like a choice we made? Revert the one change that made it. The image rebuilds without it, and stays that way.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Break nothing, ever" icon="approve-check">
    The whole OS is one bootc image on a Fedora base. Updates arrive as a complete system, and a bad change is one rollback away. Experiment freely.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Know why everything is there" icon="magnifier">
    Every line traces back to a prompt, a reason and a pull request. No folklore, no forum archaeology.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Finished, fun, and stable

We are aiming for the polish of a Mac, the fun of Omarchy and the steadiness of Fedora, with zero opinions you can't remove. A small, fast core: the Mango compositor and our own Quickshell desktop shell, scaled to your monitor's real pixel density right from the first boot.

<Aside type="tip" title="Where it is going">
  A theme that follows your wallpaper across GTK and Qt apps. Shareable intents such as "battery first" or "make it feel like a 2005 Mac" that anyone can apply, adapt and revert. Your whole OS restored on a new laptop from your journal.
</Aside>

## Built in the open, one prompt at a time

Stack OS is young: the first milestone, a minimal desktop that installs from an ISO onto Btrfs, is coming together now. Every step is published with its goal, its expectations, the exact prompt and the result, each linked to its commit. Watch it grow, or build your own alongside it.
